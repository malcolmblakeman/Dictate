Hot-seat rules
Two players, one board. You choose what they must place; they place it, then choose what you must place. The AI opponent in Controls plays the selected mode and side. Advanced 2×2 mode also lets any contiguous 2×2 block win. Opposite Mode reverses it: whoever completes a shape loses. Four in a row, column, or diagonal wins if all four pieces share on at least one trait.

How to play Dictate

There are 16 pieces with four binary traits: solid or dashed, red or blue, square or circle, and triangle or empty.

On your turn you give your opponent one unused piece. They must place it on an empty square. If it completes a winning shape, they win. Otherwise they give you a piece, and play continues.

• Advanced 2×2

Advanced 2×2 is optional: classic Dictate uses the same 10 rows, columns, and diagonals. Advanced 2×2 keeps those 10 and adds the 9 contiguous 2×2 blocks, so any 2×2 block also wins when all four pieces share at least one trait. That makes 19 possible winning shapes. Switching modes resets the board.

• AI opponent

AI opponent plays whichever mode is selected: turn on AI opponent in Controls and pick its side — Player 1 (it moves first), Player 2 (you move first), or Both to watch it play itself. It takes immediate wins, never hands you a piece you could win with right away (and when defeat is forced, it concedes the fewest simultaneous wins), blocks three-in-a-row threats, and searches the endgame exactly with alpha-beta pruning when few pieces remain. Levels: Easy plays casually and donates carelessly, Normal thinks ahead, Hard searches deeper. Switching modes or sides resets the board.

• Opposite Mode

Opposite Mode is misère Dictate: whoever completes a winning shape LOSES, so you win by forcing your opponent to finish a line — or a 2×2 block in Advanced mode. Draws work the same: if no shape can ever be completed, the game is drawn.

• Puzzles

Puzzles (🧩 top-right): hand-checked positions built from real engine analysis — spot the winning placement, find the only safe donation, escape a misère trap, or deliver a killer gift. Wrong attempts are taken back automatically; solves are counted in tries.

Controls: Z/U undo, R reset, E eval bar, L light or dark, I or ? info, Esc close.
